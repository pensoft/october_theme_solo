Public review & impact assessment.

Two flagship outputs from the SOLO consortium, open for the wider Soil Mission community.

Roadmaps Review

The SOLO Think Tanks have developed key research and innovation roadmaps addressing critical soil knowledge gaps, and they are now ready for public review.

Impact Assessment

This comprehensive framework offers adaptable tools, metrics, and narratives to evaluate the actual impact of Mission Soil projects across policy, economy, and society.

Regionalisation

Regionalisation bridges the gap between the broader EU goals and the different local cases by translating the Soil Mission objectives into context-specific solutions for the individual regions. By drawing on the insights gathered from our Soil Weeks and Regional Nodes, we ensure that research and policy effectively address the diverse environmental and agricultural needs of local communities.
